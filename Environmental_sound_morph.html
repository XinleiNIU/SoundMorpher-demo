SoundMorpher: Perceptually-Uniform Sound Morphing with Diffusion Model

Enviromental Sound Morphing

This page contains demonstration of environmental sound morphing.

This section we demonstrate how SoundMorpher smoothly morph source environmental sound to the target environmental sound cross different categories. Source and target audio recordings are from ESC50 dataset and AudioCaps dateset. Please note that this section also includes failure cases with abrupt transitions for SoundMorpher that two input sounds as significant semantic and temporal content differences which discussed in limitation section.
